FAQ

Straight answers.

Anything else, ask on the call.

What does ScaleLab AI do?

ScaleLab AI builds and operates customer acquisition systems. Depending on where growth is stuck, that covers finding prospects, starting conversations, qualifying interest, booking meetings and tracking which activity produced each opportunity.

Is this just cold email?

No. Sending is one layer of nine. Before anything goes out, the target market is defined and prospect data is researched, verified and enriched. After it goes out, replies are detected and classified, qualified prospects are routed to a calendar, and every meeting is tied back to the campaign that produced it.

Do you work on inbound leads as well?

Yes. For businesses that already generate inquiries, ScaleLab builds the response, qualification, follow-up, scheduling and CRM routing around them, including automated phone and chat response where it fits.

Can you build custom software or a CRM?

Yes, when the acquisition problem needs it: CRM workflows, dashboards, integrations, lead routing, reporting and internal tools. Scope, integrations, timing and pricing are agreed after discovery.

How is pricing structured?

It depends on the system and scope. Engagements can combine implementation with performance-based pricing tied to clearly defined outcomes, such as qualified opportunities or meetings. Not every engagement is performance-based, and terms are agreed before work begins.

What types of businesses do you work with?

Businesses whose growth depends on new customer conversations and meetings, such as staffing agencies, trades and home-service companies, and B2B service firms. The strategy call confirms whether the market and offer are a fit.

How long does it take to launch?

It depends on scope. A focused system can launch quickly, while larger builds follow a staged process. Every system is tested end to end before it goes live.
