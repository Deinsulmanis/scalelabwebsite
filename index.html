FAQ

Common Questions
Answered Directly

What does an AI receptionist do for my business?

An AI receptionist automatically answers customer enquiries, qualifies leads, and books appointments 24/7 via phone, chat, or SMS. It ensures no lead goes unanswered after hours, on weekends, or while you're busy with another customer.

How long does it take to get a landing page built?

Most lead-capture landing pages are live within 5 business days of your strategy call. ScaleLab AI builds custom, mobile-first landing pages for businesses in New Westminster and across Canada — from design to launch in under a week.

Do you work with businesses outside of New Westminster?

Yes — ScaleLab AI is remote-first and serves businesses across Canada. While based in New Westminster, BC, we work with clients in Vancouver, Burnaby, Surrey, Coquitlam, and throughout the country.

How much do your services cost?

Pricing depends on the scope of your project. ScaleLab AI offers a free 30-minute strategy call where we outline exactly what to build and provide transparent pricing — no commitment required.

Can the AI receptionist handle enquiries after hours?

Yes — the AI receptionist operates 24/7, including evenings, weekends, and holidays. It responds to customer enquiries instantly at any hour and routes qualified leads directly to your team.

What types of businesses does ScaleLab AI work with?

ScaleLab AI works with service-based businesses including real estate agents, med spas, home services, legal practices, and any business that relies on customer bookings or enquiries. If you lose leads after hours, we can help.

What is the difference between Lead Generation and an AI Receptionist?

An AI receptionist handles inbound calls, chat, and SMS enquiries in real time, while lead generation proactively reaches out to build your pipeline with new prospects. Together they cover both sides of growing your business — inbound and outbound.
