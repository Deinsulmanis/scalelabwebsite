FAQ

Common Questions
Answered Directly

What does ScaleLab AI build?

ScaleLab AI builds custom business software and CRM systems, AI receptionists, automated lead systems, and conversion-focused websites for service businesses.

Can you build a custom CRM?

Yes. The CRM is designed around your sales and operational workflow, with scope, integrations, timing, and pricing determined after discovery.

Does the AI receptionist answer phone calls?

Yes. It can handle phone calls and supported messaging channels, answer common questions, qualify callers, and book appointments according to your business's configuration.

How long does a project take?

Timelines depend on scope. Focused websites or automations may launch quickly, while custom systems follow a staged design, build, and testing process.

Do you replace existing software?

We first evaluate your current workflow and can integrate with, improve, or replace existing tools when a custom system is justified.

What businesses do you work with?

We focus on service businesses, including dental practices, med spas, and home-service companies, while working with other suitable businesses that manage inquiries, appointments, customers, and follow-up.
