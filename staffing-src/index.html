Questions

Straight answers.

Anything else, ask on the call.

Is this candidate sourcing?

No. We don’t source, screen or place candidates. Your recruiters keep doing that. We work on employer acquisition: finding companies that need the workers you place and getting them into a meeting with your team.

Who are you contacting?

Employers that fit the roles, industries and geography we agree with you up front, and the people at those companies responsible for hiring or staffing decisions.

What counts as a qualified employer meeting?

The employer fits the agreed market and has relevant roles or a staffing need, the contact is an appropriate decision-maker or influencer with genuine interest in discussing staffing, and the meeting actually takes place. We agree on the definition before the campaign begins.

Do we need to change our recruiting process?

No. Nothing changes on the recruiting side. You take the meetings and handle each staffing requirement the way you already do.

What does ScaleLab handle?

Employer targeting, decision-maker identification, prospecting, personalized outreach, follow-up, reply handling, qualification, and booking meetings onto your calendar.

What happens if no qualified meetings are generated?

Then there are no meeting fees. We get paid based on the meetings we generate.

Do we need to manage the outreach ourselves?

No. We run it from start to finish. Your team agrees the target market with us and takes the meetings.

Will this work with the roles we already specialize in?

It’s built around them. The pilot starts with the roles you already place, such as welders, machinists, CNC operators, millwrights or production workers, and targets employers that hire for those roles. We’ll confirm on the call whether your market is a fit.
